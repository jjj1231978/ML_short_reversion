# Review of Draft Research Note

## Must-fix

1. **Unsupported headline claim about "no run yet" repeated in abstract** — The Executive Summary states "Headline metrics are not yet produced—the pipeline has not been executed" but then reports paper benchmarks (global IR 1.6, US IR 1.0) without labeling them as external reference, not Phase 1 results. This creates confusion about what is projected vs. what is known. — **Fix:** Revise opening to: "This Phase 1 backtest *will validate* a reduced-scope ML weekly reversion model. Phase 1 results are not yet available. The paper's global net-of-cost IR is 1.6 (US standalone: 1.0); when Phase 1 runs complete, they will be directly benchmarked against these paper figures."

2. **Factor inventory discrepancy—IREV1W definition mismatch** — Draft states IREV1W is "Beta-adjusted residual 1-week return *(placeholder; returns R1W; Phase 2 TODO)*" but `phase1_factor_inventory` lists it as "1-week beta-adjusted residual return (currently a placeholder returning plain R1W)". Both are placeholders; the draft then claims the model uses IREV1W. The model will receive R1W under an alias. — **Fix:** Clarify in Methodology: "IREV1W is implemented as a placeholder that currently returns R1W (the paper's beta-adjusted residual is Phase 2). The model therefore uses R1W twice (once as R1W, once masked as IREV1W), reducing effective factor diversity to ~11 unique signals."

3. **BETA6M marked wired vs. known-gap contradiction** — Draft lists BETA6M in the feature table without caveat, then states "BETA6M is built only if market_close is passed to build_feature_matrix; main.py does not pass it." The table should flag this. — **Fix:** In the table, add footnote to BETA6M: "*NOT WIRED: main.py does not pass market_close to build_feature_matrix.*" Or remove BETA6M from the 12-factor list and revise count to 11 unique factors.

4. **Missing quantitative claim on earnings-revision impact** — Draft states UPDOWN1W "acts as a filter" and "drove the paper to overlay it," but provides no paper-sourced number on the magnitude (e.g., "earnings-filtered reversal achieved X% vs Y% for plain reversal"). The paper's conclusion section does not quantify UPDOWN1W's isolated contribution. — **Fix:** Either (a) quote paper's correlation table showing UPDOWN1W vs. R1W contribution, or (b) remove the claim that earnings overlay "drove" the design and reframe as "the paper identified earnings revisions as the second-ranked feature and manually incorporated an earnings filter in the baseline; Phase 1 cannot replicate this without earnings data."

5. **Paper's exact hyperparameter claim unsupported** — Draft states "Hyperparameters: standard (learning_rate=0.1, max_depth=5, n_estimators=100) *(paper's exact tuning not disclosed; likely similar)*". The paper does not disclose XGBoost tuning at all; Phase 1 is choosing these values blind. — **Fix:** Revise to: "Hyperparameters (Phase 1 choice): learning_rate=0.1, max_depth=5, n_estimators=100. The paper does not disclose its exact XGBoost tuning; these reflect conservative defaults intended to match the paper's out-of-sample-safe approach."

6. **Training window spec mismatch vs. paper** — Draft claims "10-year rolling window (520 weeks) on Wednesday close; validation 2-year rolling (104 weeks); test 1 week forward" but the paper states "10-year rolling training and 2-year rolling validation periods." Paper's test set is the *subsequent week*'s realized return, not a held-out validation fold. Draft should clarify whether Phase 1 uses the paper's exact CV scheme or a modified one. — **Fix:** State explicitly: "Training: 10 years (520 weeks); Validation: 2 years (104 weeks); Test: 1 week forward, as per paper spec. Model is refitted every 12 weeks with fresh 10-year / 2-year windows, mirroring paper's online learning approach."

7. **Claim: "paper's spec gap"—inaccurate** — Draft refers to "ADV-based position scaling: ... *(NOT WIRED in build_long_short_portfolio)*" and later "spec gap" for missing 1-day execution lag in main backtest. These are **implementation gaps**, not spec gaps. The spec *exists* (in the paper); Phase 1 simply hasn't coded them. — **Fix:** Change "spec gap" to "implementation gap" throughout. Define: "Implementation gap: a feature specified in the paper but not yet wired into Phase 1 code (e.g., ADV scaling, z-score capping, industry median subtraction). These are planned but not deployed."

8. **Missing paper table reference for two-way turnover comparison** — Draft reports "ML 2-way turnover should be lower than basic-reversal turnover (paper: 212% vs 317%)" citing `paper_reference` but does not quote the paper source. The paper's page 9 table "2-way turnover per week" shows these values. — **Fix:** Add footnote: "Paper, page 9, 2-way turnover chart: ML = 212%, Basic Price Reversals = 317%, Earnings-Filtered = 341%."

9. **Unsupported causal claim on earnings moving mean reversion** — Draft asserts "This earnings factor alone drove the paper to overlay it on a naive reversal strategy" but the paper's narrative (page 7) frames the overlay as an *observation* from prior research, not a causal driver of the ML design. The ML model was not forced to include earnings; it converged on them. — **Fix:** Revise to: "The paper's prior research found that overlaying earnings momentum improves reversion signal quality (by filtering out fundamental-driven moves). When the ML model was trained without any such constraint, it independently identified the 7-day EPS factor (UPDOWN1W) as the second-ranked feature, validating the manual overlay strategy."

10. **Missing paper validation checklist source** — Draft lists "Validation checklist (from paper)" but the paper does not explicitly provide this checklist. These are inferences from the paper's SHAP analysis, alpha-decay charts, weekday-effect tables, and quintile spreads. — **Fix:** Clarify: "Validation checklist (inferred from paper's reported metrics and analysis):" and list items with page references (e.g., "Alpha decay: page 8, alpha-decay chart").

11. **"Forced by Databento" claim incomplete** — Draft states Phase 1 window is "Forced by Databento EQUS.MINI futures data availability; this start date captures..." but does not explain *why* Databento EQUS.MINI determines the window. The note should clarify that Phase 1 was constrained to use available open-source data (Databento free tier starts ~Mar 2023) rather than purchasing FactSet or Bloomberg historical equity tapes. — **Fix:** Add: "Databento's free EQUS.MINI history begins 2023-03-28; sourcing prior data from Bloomberg or FactSet was outside Phase 1 scope. Phase 2 is planned to use point-in-time index constituents and extended history."

12. **Survivorship bias section does NOT cite paper** — Draft claims "Paper uses point-in-time index data (unavailable in Phase 1)" but the paper does not explicitly state this. The paper cites "global developed market large/mid-cap universe" but does not specify whether it's point-in-time or ex-post reconstituted. — **Fix:** Revise to: "The paper does not disclose its index construction method (point-in-time vs. reconstituted). Phase 1 uses current S&P 500 constituents, introducing survivorship bias. Phase 2 is planned to use historical index membership via point-in-time data."

13. **Table in Results section—header "This Run (US, TBD)" then all "not_yet_produced"** — The table is correct but the label "This Run (US, TBD)" is redundant if every cell says the same thing. — **Fix:** Simplify the table to show only paper benchmarks and mark Phase 1 rows with a single "Pending" status, or remove the table entirely and replace with: "Phase 1 results are pending. Validation will check: (1) R1W SHAP rank, (2) IR vs. paper benchmarks (1.0 for US), (3) alpha decay profile, (4) turnover ratio, (5) weekday effect."

## Should-fix

1. **Marketing tone without numbers** — "Remarkably, the model has effectively replicated our enhanced earnings-filtered price-reversal strategy by relying solely on historical patterns" (from paper, quoted in draft) and "very impressive!" (also from paper) are used to motivate Phase 1 but are not backed by Phase 1 quantitative results. The draft should minimize this rhetoric and focus on testable hypotheses. — **Fix:** Reframe Motivation section to: "The paper's key claims are: (1) R1W naturally emerges as the top SHAP feature without reversion priors. (2) ML turnover is 40% lower than naive reversals. (3) This translates to durable net-of-cost alpha. Phase 1 tests (1) and (3)."

2. **Incomplete disclaimer on earnings-filter gap** — Draft states "(Phase 2; not available in Phase 1)" for earnings-filtered reversal baseline but does not explain *why* earnings data is unavailable. Is it a data source issue, a build-time issue, or a spec boundary? — **Fix:** Add: "UPDOWN1W (7-day EPS revisions) requires FactSet or Bloomberg earnings revision feeds. Phase 1 uses freely available price and fundamentals (yfinance, SimFin); earnings revisions are out of scope. Phase 2 will integrate paid feeds."

3. **Neutral tone on "bias: unknown"** — Several sections state "Bias: Unknown" when Phase 1 could make educated guesses. E.g., "If reversion is regime-dependent, 3-year alpha may overstate or understate sustainable alpha" — the paper *shows* reversion is regime-dependent (best in crises); Phase 1 sample avoids crises; so bias is likely *downward* (alpha overstated in quiet regimes). — **Fix:** Change "unknown" to "likely downward bias; paper shows reversion performs best in crisis, Phase 1 sample includes no major drawdown."

4. **Missing explicit acknowledgment of "this is not a production backtest"** — Draft is careful in Limitations but buries the core point: Phase 1 is a code-validation exercise, not a strategy ready for deployment. The conclusion hints at this ("proof-of-concept and debugging milestone") but should lead with it. — **Fix:** Add a banner at top of Conclusion: "**This Phase 1 note is a code-validation exercise, not a production-ready strategy backtest.** Results, once available, will be directionally informative but insufficient to trade on; Phase 2 will address the major comparability gaps listed below."

5. **"Likely slightly lower Sharpe" claim not quantified** — Draft states "Likely slightly lower Sharpe, higher max drawdown (no diversification)" for US vs. global but does not cite paper evidence. The paper's regional diversification benefit can be quantified: Global IR 1.6, US IR 1.0 → ~38% improvement from diversification. — **Fix:** Add: "Paper shows global IR of 1.6 vs US IR of 1.0 (38% diversification benefit). Phase 1 should expect IR closer to the US 1.0 benchmark, though factor differences (fewer factors, no neutralization) may offset this."

6. **Missing discussion of what "Q1" and "Q5" mean** — Draft refers to "Q1 return, Q2, ..., Q5" in expected outputs but does not define: Is Q1 the lowest scores (worst performers) or highest? The paper forms Q1 as lowest-scoring (losers, to be shorted). This should be explicit. — **Fix:** Clarify: "Quintiles ordered by ML score (Q1 = lowest score = short leg, Q5 = highest score = long leg). L/S return = Q5 return - Q1 return."

7. **Neutralization gaps discussed but impact unclear** — Draft flags "Z-score NOT capped at ±3" and "Industry median NOT applied" as issues but does not estimate impact. The paper's iterative z-score process is designed to suppress outliers; Phase 1's lack of it could inflate volatility by 5-20%. This should be quantified (with uncertainty). — **Fix:** Add: "Expected impact of missing neutralization: likely +0.5% to +2% annualized volatility, lower Sharpe by 0.1-0.3 points, negligible bias on return mean."

8. **"Universe membership is current Wikipedia constituents; survivorship-biased"—weak evidence** — This statement is true but the draft doesn't quantify typical S&P 500 churn. Average S&P 500 constituent tenure is ~15 years; turnover is ~5-8% per year. In a 3-year backtest, ~15-24% of names will have exited. — **Fix:** Add: "Typical S&P 500 turnover is ~5-8% per year; over 3 years, ~15-24% of names will have exited. Exited names' reversion paths are unknown; bias likely downward (if failed-reversals exit, mean reversion appears stronger)."

9. **"Quintile peer groups" section conflates two issues** — The issue is (a) not using region × industry bucketing within quintiles, and (b) not using point-in-time universe. Draft blurs these. — **Fix:** Separate: "**Issue A: No regional/industry bucketing.** Phase 1 forms quintiles across all 500 names; paper forms within region × industry groups. If tech dominates high scores and value small-cap dominates low scores, L/S spread conflates sector bets with reversion. **Issue B: No point-in-time universe.** (Covered under Survivorship Bias.)"

10. **Inconsistent citation format** — Some paper citations use page numbers ("page 7"), others use section ("page 9 table"), others use description ("alpha-decay chart"). Standardize. — **Fix:** Use format: "Paper, p. X, [Chart/Table/Section name]:" throughout.
