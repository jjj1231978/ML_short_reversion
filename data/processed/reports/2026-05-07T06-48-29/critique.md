6. **"Turnover is measured as 2-way weekly position turnover (long + short legs)."** and **"The research pack reports average weekly two-way turnover of 48.4%"** — The research pack specifies `avg_weekly_turnover: 0.48371654688777715`. The research pack's 0.4837 likely means 48.37% 2-way weekly, which is **10× higher than the paper's implied weekly rate.** This is a major discrepancy that demands explanation. **Fix: State clearly—"The research pack reports 48.4% average 2-way weekly turnover. Annualized, this is 48.4% × 52 = 2,517%, vs the paper's 212% (ML) and 317%–341% (baselines). This 10× gap suggests either a unit error in the research pack, a calculation error in this run (position concentration inflating rebalancing volume), or a fundamental difference in how turnover is measured (e.g., gross position change vs. portfolio delta). This requires investigation before reporting."** Do not gloss over this with "possible scaling error."
